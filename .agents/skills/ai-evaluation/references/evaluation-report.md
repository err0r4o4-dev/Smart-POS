# Evaluation Evidence

When a report/artifact is requested, retain enough evidence to reproduce the comparison without secrets or raw private records.

- Approved objective and acceptance decision; distinguish forecast demand from observed sales.
- Data provenance/version, tenant scope, time bounds, grain, units, cutoff and missing/stockout policy.
- Method and baseline versions, preprocessing, train/test windows, horizons and reproducibility settings.
- Metric definitions, denominator/zero handling, aggregate weighting and missing-forecast treatment.
- Results by relevant horizon/segment, sample support, baseline comparison and variability.
- Supported uncertainty coverage/width when available; otherwise state that uncertainty was not evaluated.
- Checks actually executed, limitations, unavailable/insufficient-data outcomes and unresolved decisions.

Do not claim measured accuracy for an unimplemented method, infer production readiness from local backtests or execute retail actions from an evaluation result.
