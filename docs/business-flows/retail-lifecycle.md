# Retail lifecycle - target

Current status (2026-10-12): the sequence below is the target lifecycle, not a statement that only Phase0 exists today. Phase0–5 retail operations have recorded local acceptance; the subsequent online extension has source/reservation/internal payment/reconciliation/delivery-snapshot/pre-shipment-refund slices through schema23 but is **PARTIAL / PAUSED by the user**. Customer shipment/return/refund UI/runtime and channel reporting remain incomplete. See [current architecture](../architecture/overview.md), [confirmed online policy](../architecture/payment-sandbox.md#current-online-flow-status-2026-10-12) and [recorded evidence](../development/verification.md).

Store setup -> catalog/product registration -> suppliers -> purchase order draft/approval -> goods receipt -> warehouse ledger/balances -> shelf replenishment -> POS checkout -> confirmed payment -> sale and stock deduction -> receipt -> reporting -> AI forecast/reorder proposal -> human-approved purchase draft -> next cycle.

Identity/organization owns tenant and branch context. Catalog owns identifiers/units. Purchasing orchestrates receiving with inventory in one transaction. Shelves orchestrates inventory transfers. Sales coordinates pricing, payments, stock and receipts with idempotency. Reporting projects durable events; intelligence uses authorized snapshots and produces proposals only.

Unknown barcode requires registration/selection; scanning alone does not supply metadata. Shelf counts are system-recorded until physical counting or appropriate hardware confirms them. The original Phase0 checkpoint implemented foundation health/presentation only; it is historical context, not today's cumulative implementation status. Reporting and AI steps in this target still require their own implementation and acceptance.
